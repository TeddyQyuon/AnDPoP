## Full Description (4000 characters max)

**Copy-paste text (`2,080 / 4,000` chars):**

```text
AnDPoP brings AirPods battery status, listening mode switching, and hardware controls into a clean Material 3 Expressive Android companion app.

Key features
• Live battery for each earbud and the charging case, including charging indicators and recent case status
• One-tap listening mode control: Noise Cancellation, Transparency, Adaptive Audio, and Off (varies by AirPods model)
• Conversation Awareness and Adaptive Audio strength controls
• Custom press-and-hold stem actions for Left and Right buds with the "keep at least two modes" safeguard
• 3-band Custom Equalizer (Low, Mid, High) with live response curve preview
• Microphone selection, ear detection, automatic device switching, and Call Controls (mute/unmute and hang up)
• Accessibility speed pickers for press-and-hold and noise control preferences
• Seven curated color themes (Wallpaper dynamic color, Glacier, Iris, Guava, Sage, Apricot, Graphite) with Light, Dark, and System modes
• Home-screen Glance widgets for battery and listening modes, a Quick Settings tile, connection popups, and a Dynamic Island overlay

Supported devices & requirements
• AirPods (1st, 2nd, 3rd, and 4th generation, including ANC)
• AirPods Pro (1st, 2nd Lightning & USB-C, and 3rd generation)
• AirPods Max (Lightning & USB-C)
• Beats Fit Pro, Beats Studio Buds, Beats Studio Buds+, Powerbeats Pro, Powerbeats Pro 2, and Beats Solo 4 (for models supporting Apple/Beats AACP status protocols; feature availability varies by model and firmware)
• Requires Android 13 (API 33) or newer with Bluetooth Nearby Devices access

Privacy & open source
• No ads, no analytics SDKs, no third-party tracking, and no in-app purchases
• Works fully offline as a guest—no account required
• Optional account sign-in saves only your email address and remembered device metadata so you can restore preferences across profiles; you can delete your account anytime in-app or on the web
• Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, built independently from LibrePods

AnDPoP is not affiliated with Apple Inc. AirPods and Beats are trademarks of Apple Inc.
```
