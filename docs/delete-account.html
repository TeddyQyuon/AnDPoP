AnDPoP Home Privacy Policy

Delete Your AnDPoP Account

AnDPoP accounts are optional and store only your email address and authentication record in Supabase Auth. AirPods battery readings, listening-mode preferences, and themes stay strictly on your phone and are never uploaded to the cloud.

What gets deleted

  • Deleted permanently online: Your email address, password hash, authentication tokens, and internal user ID (UUID) in Supabase Auth. Zero retention period after deletion completes.
  • Local device data: On-device AirPods preferences and logs are not stored online. Delete them anytime by clearing app data or uninstalling AnDPoP on your phone.

Option 1: Delete Immediately Inside the App

  1. Open AnDPoP on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the Account icon in the top bar.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. Your online account and local profile are erased immediately.

Option 2: Delete Immediately on the Web

If you uninstalled AnDPoP or no longer have access to your Android device, sign in below to permanently erase your AnDPoP authentication account immediately over HTTPS.

Option 3: Need Help or Forgot Password?

If you signed up with Google/Apple or cannot remember your password, you can reset your password inside AnDPoP (Account → Log in → Forgot password?) or request manual assistance via github.com/TeddyQyuon/AndPoP_V_1.6.0/issues.
