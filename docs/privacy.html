Privacy Policy for AnDPoP

Effective date: 2026-11-25 · Application ID: app.andpop.android · License: GPL-3.0-or-later

1. Overview

AnDPoP is a companion app for viewing AirPods battery status, switching listening modes, and configuring hardware settings on Android. You can use AnDPoP completely offline as a guest without creating an account.

AnDPoP contains no advertisements, no third-party analytics SDKs, no automatic crash telemetry uploads, and no in-app purchases.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide Bluetooth companion functionality, AnDPoP stores the following data strictly on your phone inside app-private storage (shared_prefs/settings.xml, shared_prefs/packet_logs.xml, and filesDir/logs/):

  • Device & Bluetooth metadata: Connected AirPods device name, model number, firmware strings, serial numbers, Bluetooth MAC address, and Bluetooth proximity/encryption keys needed for local BLE battery updates.
  • Preferences: Listening mode preferences, stem press-and-hold actions, Accessibility settings, equalizer tuning, theme and appearance choices, and per-profile snapshots.
  • Optional diagnostic logs: If you explicitly start a recording in Settings → Troubleshooting, AnDPoP captures its own process Bluetooth logs locally with Bluetooth addresses truncated to their last two bytes. Logs stay on your phone until you choose to share or delete them.

AnDPoP explicitly excludes settings.xml, packet_logs.xml, and logs/ from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer (backup_rules.xml and data_extraction_rules.xml).

3. Optional Online Account

Signing in is optional. If you choose to create or sign into an AnDPoP account via the configured Supabase authentication service:

  • Data collected: Your email address and internal authentication user ID (UUID).
  • Purpose: Account management (sign-in, email verification, password reset) and keeping separate local profiles/remembered device preferences on your device. Device settings and battery readings remain on your phone and are not synced to the cloud.
  • Encryption at rest: Session tokens, user ID, and email are encrypted on-device with Android Keystore (AES/GCM/NoPadding) inside noBackupFilesDir/andpop-account-v1, which is excluded from cloud backups.
  • Encryption in transit: All communication with the authentication service uses HTTPS (TLS) exclusively (cleartextTrafficPermitted="false").
  • Third-party sharing: AnDPoP does not sell or share your email address or personal data with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties. Authentication requests are processed by the Supabase project backend (btfbwwnbkarlpcqmkzzb.supabase.co).

4. Permissions Explained

  • Nearby devices (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN with neverForLocation): Required to communicate with paired AirPods over Bluetooth and read battery/listening-mode state. Never used to infer physical location.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Optional; displays active AirPods battery and quick mode controls in the notification shade.
  • Phone (READ_PHONE_STATE, ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS): Optional; used only if enabled to support call audio handover and nod/shake call controls.
  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Optional; used only if you enable the connection popup or Dynamic Island overlay.

5. Account & Data Deletion

  • Immediate in-app deletion: Open AnDPoP → Account → Delete account while signed in. Confirming permanently deletes your online account via delete_my_andpop_account and wipes the associated local profile.
  • Immediate web deletion: Visit the AnDPoP Delete Account page to permanently delete your online account directly from a web browser without needing the app installed.
  • Local data deletion: Clear app storage or uninstall AnDPoP in Android Settings to remove all locally stored settings and logs, or delete individual recordings in Troubleshooting.

6. Children's Privacy

AnDPoP is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
